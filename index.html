How to Use the Gold Valuation Calculator

This calculator helps you estimate gold values, scheme values, eligible amounts and monthly interest. Enter your information in the editable fields; calculated fields update automatically.

1. ENTER BANK GOLD RATE

Enter the current Bank Gold Rate per gram in the Bank Gold Rate field.

Example: Bank Gold Rate = ₹13,500 per gram

The calculator automatically uses this rate for the Agriculture Scheme and Retail Scheme.

2. ENTER GOLD DETAILS

The Gold Valuation Table contains six rows for entering individual gold items.

  • Gross Weight (g): Enter the total weight of the gold item.
  • Deduction (g): Enter the weight to be deducted from the gross weight.
  • User Value: Enter the value used by the calculator for the total net-weight calculation.
  • 22K (90%) and 18K (80%): These values are calculated automatically.

3. CALCULATE NET WEIGHT

Net Weight = Gross Weight − Deduction

Example: 10.000 g − 1.000 g = 9.000 g

If the deduction is greater than the gross weight, the calculated net weight does not become negative; it uses 0 instead.

4. CALCULATE 22K WEIGHT

The calculator takes 90% of the Net Weight for the 22K calculation.

22K Weight = Net Weight × 90%

The remaining 10% is treated as a deduction for wastage.

Note: We take 90% and consider the remaining 10% as wastage for this calculation.

* Not necessarily to be taken in hallmark cases.

Example: 9.000 × 0.90 = 8.100 g

5. CALCULATE 18K WEIGHT

The calculator takes 80% of the Net Weight for the 18K calculation.

18K Weight = Net Weight × 80%

The remaining 20% is treated as a deduction because we convert 18K gold to 22K.

Note: We take 80% and consider the remaining 20% as deduction because we convert 18K gold to 22K.

Example: 9.000 × 0.80 = 7.200 g

6. TOTAL NET WEIGHT

The TOTAL NET WEIGHT is calculated from the User Value entries. It does not add the displayed 22K or 18K columns.

Total Net Weight = Sum of all User Value entries

Example: 10.00 + 5.00 + 7.50 + 2.50 = 25.00 g

7. AGRICULTURE SCHEME

The Agriculture Scheme contains three calculation levels: 75%, 80% and 85%.

Rate Per Gram = Bank Gold Rate × Scheme %
Eligible Amount = Total Net Weight × Bank Gold Rate × Scheme %

Example with ₹13,500/g and 25 g:

  • 75% = ₹10,125/g; Eligible Amount = ₹253,125
  • 80% = ₹10,800/g; Eligible Amount = ₹270,000
  • 85% = ₹11,475/g; Eligible Amount = ₹286,875

8. RETAIL SCHEME

The Retail Scheme contains two calculation levels: 65% and 68%.

Rate Per Gram = Bank Gold Rate × Scheme %
Eligible Amount = Total Net Weight × Bank Gold Rate × Scheme %

Example with ₹13,500/g and 25 g:

  • 65% = ₹8,775/g; Eligible Amount = ₹219,375
  • 68% = ₹9,180/g; Eligible Amount = ₹229,500

9. ENTER FINAL ELIGIBLE AMOUNT

Enter the actual eligible loan/amount that you want to use for the interest calculation.

Example: Final Eligible Amount = ₹250,000

This amount is used for calculating monthly interest.

Important: Enter the Final Eligible Amount and the respective ROI to get the per-month interest rate (%) and per-month interest amount.

10. ENTER ROI

Enter the applicable ROI (Rate of Interest) as a percentage.

Example: Annual ROI = 12%

11. CALCULATE INTEREST PER MONTH

Interest Per Month (%) = Annual ROI ÷ 12

Example: 12 ÷ 12 = 1% per month

Therefore, Interest Per Month = 1.0000%

12. CALCULATE MONTHLY INTEREST AMOUNT

Interest Amount Month = Final Eligible Amount × (ROI ÷ 12) ÷ 100

Example: ₹250,000 × (12 ÷ 12) ÷ 100 = ₹2,500

13. AGRICULTURE ROI SLABS

  • Up to ₹2.5 L: Use the applicable ROI up to ₹2.5 lakh.
  • Above ₹2.5 L: Use the applicable ROI above ₹2.5 lakh.

14. RETAIL ROI SLABS

  • Up to ₹10 L: Use the applicable ROI up to ₹10 lakh.
  • Above ₹10 L: Use the applicable ROI above ₹10 lakh.

15. RECOMMENDED ORDER OF USE

  1. Enter the Bank Gold Rate per gram.
  2. Enter Gross Weight for each gold item.
  3. Enter the applicable Deduction.
  4. Enter the User Value.
  5. Check the automatically calculated 22K (90%) value.
  6. Check the automatically calculated 18K (80%) value.
  7. Check the Total Net Weight.
  8. Review the Agriculture Scheme values.
  9. Review the Retail Scheme values.
  10. Enter the Final Eligible Amount.
  11. Enter the applicable ROI.
  12. Check the Interest Per Month.
  13. Check the Interest Amount Month.

All calculations update automatically as you enter or change values.

16. CLEAR TABLE

The CLEAR TABLE button clears the editable fields in the Gold Valuation Table:

  • Gross Weight
  • Deduction
  • User Value

It does not change the calculator's formulas.

17. IMPORTANT CALCULATION SUMMARY

18. QUICK REFERENCE

Bank Gold Rate → Gross Weight → Deduction → Net Weight → 22K/18K → User Value → Total Net Weight → Scheme Value → Final Eligible Amount + Respective ROI → Per-Month Interest Rate + Per-Month Interest Amount

Tip: Enter or edit the values from top to bottom. All calculated results update instantly without reloading the page.
